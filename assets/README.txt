PIXEL PET
=========
A tiny pixel pet that lives on your desktop and helps you get things done.

Getting started
  - Run pixel-pet.exe. The pet appears in the bottom-right corner and introduces itself.
  - Right-click the pet to open its hub: Feed, Trick, Tasks, Focus timer, Stats, Pets, Options.
  - Stroke the pet with the mouse to make it happy, click to hit it, pull it, or pick it up and throw it.
  - Throw its ball and it fetches; choose a friend for it under Pets; hats are in the Wardrobe.
  - Drop a file, folder or shortcut on the pet to add it to its quick launch.
  - Looks > Packs imports more characters (.petpack files); so does dropping one on the pet.
  - Feed it when it is hungry: food for the animals and people, fuel for the machines.
  - Walking, random tricks, gravity and what a left click does are under Settings > Behaviour.

Hotkeys (Settings > Keys and startup > Shortcut keys changes them)
  Ctrl+Alt+T   add a task
  Ctrl+Alt+F   toggle focus mode (pomodoro timer)
  Ctrl+Alt+S   open the stats window
  Ctrl+Alt+M   toggle minimal mode (small and quiet; hover for details)
  Ctrl+Alt+H   hide or show the pet (so does clicking the tray icon)
  Ctrl+Alt+P   open the pet hub
  Ctrl+Alt+E   feed the pet its favourite
  Ctrl+Alt+K   calculator
  Ctrl+Alt+N   notes
  Ctrl+Alt+D   open VS Code
  Ctrl+Alt+Q   close the pet (and open it, if switched on in Settings > Keys and startup)

Smart tasks
  Type naturally: "call mom tomorrow 5pm", "stretch in 45m", "standup weekdays 9:30",
  "team sync every monday 10am".

Privacy
  Everything stays on your PC. No account, no telemetry, no network access except reading
  your own network speed counters. Settings and data live in %APPDATA% (files starting with
  "pixel-pet"). To uninstall, delete the exe and those files.

Notes
  The pet hides itself for games, films and fullscreen apps (Settings > Hiding).
  Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unsigned app: choose "More info" > "Run anyway".
