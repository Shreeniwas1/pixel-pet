Windows says the app is unrecognized. Is it safe?

Pixel Pet is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn on first run. Choose More info, then Run anyway. It has no account system and makes no network connections beyond reading your own speed counters.

Will it get in the way of games?

It hides itself while a fullscreen game or video is in front. You can switch that off in Options.

Where is my data stored?

In files starting with pixel-pet in your %APPDATA% folder. Nothing leaves your computer.

How do I uninstall it?

Close the pet, then delete the exe and those files in %APPDATA%.
